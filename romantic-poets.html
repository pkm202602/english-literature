The Sublime and the Storm: Nature in British Romantic Poetry

Published by English Literature Blog

Introduction

The Romantic era marked a profound shift in how writers perceived the natural world, moving away from Enlightenment rationalism toward an intense emotional engagement with the wilderness.

Key Writers and Themes

  • William Wordsworth: Emphasized nature as a moral teacher and a source of spiritual renewal.
  • Percy Bysshe Shelley: Explored radical politics and the untamed power of elemental forces.
  • John Keats: Focused on beauty, transience, and sensory imagery tied to the natural seasons.

Conclusion

Through their lyrical exploration of landscapes, these poets redefined the relationship between humanity and the environment.
